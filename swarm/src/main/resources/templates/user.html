Know These Before You Join

Welcome to Gamified Decision-Making. Here’s what you need to know before entering a discussion:

📌 Important Note
  • To maintain discussion integrity, please join exactly at the scheduled time (no late entry) and remain active until the session concludes.
🎯 What You’ll Do
  • Answer questions using sliders linked to options.
  • Your inputs combine with others represents the group’s decision.
  • Read the description of the discussion to understand the context, if provided by the organizer.
🕹️ How to Play
  • Higher slider value = stronger support for that option.
  • Submit as often as you like during a round.
  • If confident, keep submitting continuously to pull the decision toward your choice.
⏳ Rounds
  • Each question may have multiple rounds.
  • Weaker options drop, stronger ones continue.
  • Slider submissions reset each round — you must re-engage.
💬 Chat
  • Use live chat to discuss and coordinate with others.
  • Stay respectful, relevant and focused.
✅ Tips
  • Stay active — one submission won’t be enough.
  • Be strategic — adapt if your top option weakens.
  • Remember: this isn’t a simple vote, it’s a dynamic tug-of-war.
❌ Warnings
  • Do not refresh the page or leave the page, in that case you will be permanently out of the session.

👉 Once inside, use your sliders and chat to help shape the group’s final decision.

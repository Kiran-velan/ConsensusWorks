About the Platform

Organizer Portal Participant Portal

This is an interactive platform designed to capture collective intelligence from groups. Instead of relying on traditional voting or averaging opinions, it models how individuals continuously adjust their preferences to align with others — resulting in a truly collaborative consensus.

Our system uses the Bradley-Terry model to interpret group preferences through paired comparisons, dynamically refining the strength of each option as participants interact. The result is an evolving understanding of which ideas the group values most — driven not by noise, but by collective reasoning.

How & Why It Works

Participants engage with one question at a time, each offering multiple options. As they rate or adjust their sliders, the system continuously evaluates preferences using the Bradley–Terry framework, estimating the likelihood that each option best represents the collective choice.

The Bradley–Terry model is a statistical framework that quantifies preferences by comparing pairs of options. It assigns a strength parameter to each option, representing its relative appeal to the group. As participants interact, the model adjusts these strength parameters based on the choices made, leading to a stable consensus.

This collective evolution mirrors swarm intelligence in nature: many independent participants interact, adapt, and influence one another’s perspectives, leading to an organized and intelligent group outcome — all without any central control.

Modeling Collective Intelligence

We use the Bradley-Terry model to translate participant inputs into interpretable collective preferences, providing data-driven consensus outcomes.

Fairness through Interaction

Each participant’s voice influences the final outcome proportionally. The process rewards convergence built on shared reasoning rather than domination or bias.

Transparent Results

Visualized reports and ranking table show group preferences and which options ultimately reflect the consensus — empowering organizers with meaningful insights.

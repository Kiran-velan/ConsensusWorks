Step 1: Create a Discussion

This is where everything begins. You define the topic, add questions, and prepare the options that participants will evaluate.

  • Choose a title and description that explain the purpose of your discussion.
  • Add clear, focused questions—each should ask about one decision. Provide multiple options for each question.
  • Choose the model, Ranking (no constraints on sum of preference) or Absolute (constrained to sum of preference <= 100) on how you want the participants to play the game.
  • Set the schedule and how many options persist into the next round.
  • Once saved, share the Discussion Name and the generated Pass Key with your participants which will be available on the My Discussions.

Step 2: Manage My Discussions

Every discussion you create is saved in your dashboard. From here you can refine, remove, or review them.

  • Update a discussion if you want to adjust questions or options before it begins.
  • Delete discussions you no longer need.
  • Report gives deeper insights into the result of the discussion.
  • Duration represents the maximum total time interval required for the discussion to complete.

Step 3: Run the Discussion Game

When the session starts, participants join and the system takes care of the rest. Here's what happens:

  • Questions appear one at a time, with at most six options shown in each round.
  • Participants use sliders to submit preferences.
  • A live visualization shows the group's convergence in real-time.
  • At the end of each round, top option is revealed.
  • Once all rounds finish, the strongest option is stored as the final answer.
  • The one-minute waiting room ensures all participants are ready simultaneously for a synchronized start.
  • To maintain discussion integrity, please join exactly at the scheduled time (no late entry) and remain active until the session concludes.

Step 4: Review Results and Reports

After the session, understand outcomes with comprehensive analytics:

  • Reports – Analytics: option rankings, and a clear summary of engagement stats.
  • Export Data – Download reports and share with others.

Step 5: Tips for Better Discussions

A few practices to ensure high-quality outcomes:

  • Keep each question focused on one decision.
  • Provide options that are diverse but fair.
  • Use persisting options smartly to keep strong candidates alive.
  • Encourage participants to use the chat constructively.
  • Use reports to learn what worked and improve your future sessions.
